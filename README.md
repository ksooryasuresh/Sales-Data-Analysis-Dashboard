# Sales-Data-Analysis-Dashboard

### Tools: Excel, SQL, Power BI

## Project Summary

- Cleaned, consolidated and analysed sales data using SQL and Excel (functions, Pivot Tables, charts, summary reports) to evaluate sales trends and product, category and monthly performance.
- Built interactive Power BI dashboards and KPI reports covering revenue, sales trends, products and categories.
- Identified top-performing products and categories and recurring monthly sales patterns to support data-driven decisions.

## Business Questions

1.What are the total revenue, profit and profit margin?
2.Which products and categories perform best?
3.Which regions bring in the most sales?
4.Are there monthly or seasonal sales patterns?
5.Who are the top customers?

## Dataset

- Columns: OrderID, OrderDate, CustomerName, Region, Product, Category, Quantity, UnitPrice, Sales, Cost, Profit
- 9 products, 2 categories (Electronics, Furniture), 4 regions (East, North, South, West), 100 named customers
- Sales = Quantity × UnitPrice, and Profit = Sales − Cost

## Dashboard Features

- KPI cards: Revenue, Profit, Profit Margin, Orders, Average Order Value, Units Sold
- Filters: Category, Region and Period (by decade)
- Switch between Revenue, Profit and Orders
- Click a product to filter the whole dashboard
- Charts: yearly trend, category split, sales by region, sales by product, monthly pattern
- Tables: product profitability and top customers
- Light and dark mode, works on mobile


## Key Insights

### Products

- Monitor has the highest revenue (12.87M), but the lowest margin (5.02%).
- Laptop makes the most profit (706.5K, 6.34% margin), followed by Bookshelf (689.4K, 6.08%).
- Smartphone has the lowest revenue (9.92M) and profit (543.4K).
- 41% of Monitor revenue comes from the East region.

### Categories

- Furniture: 47.77M (50.6%). Electronics: 46.60M (49.4%).
- Both have almost the same margin (5.43% vs 5.45%).

### Regions

- East has the highest revenue (25.08M, 26.6%), then South, North and West.
- West has the lowest revenue but the best margin (5.97%). North has the lowest margin (5.08%).

### Monthly Patterns

- December is the best month (11.5% of revenue, 104 orders).
- August (10.2%) and March (9.6%) are also strong.
- September (6.3%), June (6.7%) and November (6.7%) are the weakest.
- Q4 is the biggest quarter (25.9%).
- 2024 was the best year (2.83M). 2025 data only goes to March.

## Recommendations

1.Review Monitor pricing and cost. A 1% margin increase on Monitors adds about 129K profit.
2.Promote Laptops and Bookshelves, which have the highest margins.
3.Increase stock and marketing before December and August.
4.Run campaigns to improve sales in June, September and November.
5.Apply West region pricing practices to the North region.
6.Improve data entry: 86 orders (8.6%) are missing a region, product or customer name.


## Skills Used

Data cleaning, Excel (functions, Pivot Tables, charts), SQL (GROUP BY, CTEs, window functions), Power BI (DAX, KPI cards, dashboards), data analysis and business insights.
